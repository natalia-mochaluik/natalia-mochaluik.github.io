# Selected brand: ВЗАИМНО / VZAIMNO

## Why this name

«Взаимно» is both the name and the operating principle of the agency. The
service does not end with a suggested profile: the team personally accompanies
people from the first sign of mutual sympathy to an organised first date.
Every introduction proceeds only when both people are interested.

**Core promise:** «ВЗАИМНО — от первой симпатии до первого свидания».

**Why clients choose the agency:** «Не просто знакомим. Помогаем взаимности
состояться».

## Mark concept

The primary emblem uses the first and last letters of the international
spelling `VZAIMNO`: `V` and `O`. They are not initials or an abbreviation. They
compress the full journey of the word into one sign:

- the two strokes of `V` converge into a point of mutual interest;
- the line continues into `O`, closing into a shared space and representing
  the first real meeting;
- the full Cyrillic name «ВЗАИМНО» always appears beside the emblem in the
  primary lockup.

The slight pause required to understand `VO` gives the brand a memorable,
ownable sign rather than a literal two-letter abbreviation. The full Latin
spelling `VZAIMNO` may replace the Cyrillic wordmark in future China-facing or
international communication while the emblem remains unchanged.

## Visual character

- Deep graphite and muted moss olive on warm ivory.
- Mature, restrained and contemporary.
- No hearts, rings, profiles, wedding symbols, pink, red or decorative
  romantic clichés.

# Earlier naming shortlist

## Criteria

- Mature and trustworthy rather than overtly romantic.
- No wedding clichés, diminutives or dating-app language.
- Suitable for a restrained editorial identity.
- Understandable as a standalone brand with the descriptor
  «агентство серьёзных знакомств».
- Capable of a simple Latin transliteration for international communication.

## Strongest directions

### 1. ДВОЕ / DVOE

Short, human and immediately connected with a relationship between two adults.
It gives the cleanest typographic logo and works well with the descriptor:
«агентство серьёзных знакомств · Россия — Китай».

### 2. ТАКТ / TAKT

Suggests delicacy, respect, rhythm and an attentive personal service. The name
feels restrained and premium, though it needs a descriptor to make the field
of work clear.

### 3. СОЗВУЧИЕ / SOZVUCHIE

Communicates compatibility of values and character rather than superficial
matching. Warm and intelligent, but longer in Latin transliteration.

## Additional options

- БЛИЖЕ / BLIZHE — warm, approachable and oriented toward a real meeting.
- СВОИ / SVOI — a trusted private circle and a sense of belonging.
- МЕЖДУ НАМИ / MEZHDU NAMI — confidentiality and a personal conversation.
- ТОЧКА ВСТРЕЧИ / TOCHKA VSTRECHI — immediately clear, but less distinctive
  and more descriptive than the three leading options.

## Launch note

Trademark, domain and social-handle availability still need a formal check
before public launch. The current selection is a creative brand decision, not
legal clearance.
