# Page system

## Decision

- User-selected first viewport: full-bleed photographic layout from
  `codex-clipboard-9928b21c-e1d8-4e5e-b4b2-12fcb2925850.png`.
- User-selected production hero source:
  `codex-clipboard-393cd8fb-992a-49ba-887e-3521dcdeec34.png`.
- Site model for the first release: one editorial landing page with anchored
  service sections. The content architecture remains ready to split into
  dedicated routes after real biographies, contacts, prices and legal copy are
  supplied.

## Home section system

| Section | Role | Required content | Primary action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header + hero | Explain the agency immediately | Russia/China eyebrow, explicit H1, privacy statement, full-bleed couple photograph, three trust points | Записаться на приватное интервью |
| Personal selection | Establish the value of a human-led service | No public profiles, attentive interview, offline introductions | Узнать, как всё устроено |
| Process | Make the service predictable | Interview, request definition, matching, meeting and feedback | Записаться на интервью |
| Services | Present the launch catalog | Personal matchmaking, closed club, photography and portfolio | Choose a direction |
| Russia + China | Explain international scope without clichés | Individual logistics, language and feasibility discussion | Обсудить международный запрос |
| Private club | Describe curated closed meetings | Interview-based admission, calm formats, privacy | Подать заявку в клуб |
| Photography | Show the in-team photographer service | Preparation, shoot, selection and coherent portfolio | Обсудить фотосъёмку |
| Principles | Humanise the team without invented biographies | Personal work, respect, honest boundaries | Познакомиться на интервью |
| FAQ | Resolve practical objections | Privacy, geography, guarantees, club and photography | Continue to the form |
| Private application | Collect a low-friction request | Name, contact, interest, short goal, consent | Отправить заявку |
| Footer | Utility navigation | Brand descriptor, anchors, privacy note, Russia and China | Return to top |

## Responsive frames

- `1440 × 900`: white horizontal header and one complete hero viewport.
- `1024`: reduced navigation gaps, preserved full-bleed hero.
- `768`: compact header, menu button, hero copy remains over a protected dark
  zone.
- `375`: text-first hero with a shorter display measure; actions become
  full-width, proof becomes a vertical list, content sections stack.

## Content boundaries

- Generated photographs are labelled as illustrative imagery.
- No testimonials, success rates, member counts, guarantees or verification
  claims are invented.
- Contact details, team names, exact photographer experience, prices and legal
  documents remain editable launch inputs.
