# Temporary photography asset brief

## Shared direction
- Use: website hero and service/editorial sections.
- Style: photorealistic contemporary editorial relationship photography.
- People: attractive adults approximately 35–55, confident and emotionally mature.
- Mood: quiet connection, intelligent conversation, warmth without theatrical romance.
- Wardrobe: elegant contemporary smart casual in graphite, cream, deep forest green and muted navy.
- Light: soft natural window light or restrained city evening light.
- Texture: real skin and fabric texture, light film grain, no plastic beauty retouching.
- Avoid: weddings, engagement rings as focus, hearts, flowers as romantic symbols, pink palette, hugging at sunset, exaggerated luxury, logos, text and watermarks.
- Disclosure: generated people are illustrative and must never be presented as actual clients.

## Asset 01 — Russia hero
- Couple: Russian man and Russian woman, approximately 38–45.
- Scene: modern quiet architectural interior in Moscow.
- Format: wide landscape hero with usable negative space.
